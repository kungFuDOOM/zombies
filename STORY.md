# MALL OF THE DEAD: Story Bible

## 1. Tone & Premise

90s B-movie horror with a straight face and a sick sense of humor. Scary enough to make you check
the stairwell. Fun enough to make you put in another quarter.

It is Black Friday. The mall was supposed to open at 6:00 AM. At 4:59 AM, somebody opened the
doors from the inside. What came in was not shopping.

Four strangers barricade themselves in the Security Room: a Biker, a Mall Cop, a Skater and a
Mall Goth. The only way out is through the mall, one wing at a time, and the mall has been eaten
from the inside out.

**Rules of the house:** every horror beat gets a joke within two lines, and every joke gets a
horror beat within two more. Nobody winks at the camera. The characters think this is real.

## 2. How the Outbreak Started

- **The Grease Main.** To save money, mall management replaced every stall's fryer with one shared
  oil system: a 2,000-gallon vat in the sub-basement, piped to every food counter in the building.
  The Food Court fryers, the arcade nacho pump, the theater butter dispenser, the gym smoothie bar,
  the holiday chestnut cart. One vat. One supply line. Nobody ever drains it.
- **PERMA-FRY.** Last spring, a man with no business card and a van with no windows sold management
  a drum of oil additive called PERMA-FRY. Slogan: "Never change your oil again." They never did.
  The oil started changing on its own. It got warmer at night. It started to smell like breath.
- **The doorbuster.** The Food Court's chicken counter, Cluck Hut, promised a 5:00 AM "Mega Crunch
  Bucket," first 500 customers, limit one. By 3:00 AM the line was around the building. At 4:00 AM,
  carvery manager Dale Brisket fired up Fryer Eight to hand out free samples to the line.
- **Patient zero.** Dale ate the first piece himself, to check the seasoning. He went quiet. Then he
  went red. Then he went hungry. He became **the Deep-Fry Butcher**, and the samples went out anyway.
- **4:59 AM.** Someone in the Security Room swiped a keycard and opened every front door at once.
  Five hundred people with free samples in their stomachs met five thousand people with none.
- **Why it keeps spreading.** The Grease Main is still pumping. Every wing it feeds has gone bad in
  its own way. Kill a boss, and you have only cleaned one counter.

## 3. Wing 1 Arc: Food Court to Butcher

Wing 1 is the entry point and the tutorial: it teaches the four survivors, the cutscene rhythm,
the choice timer and the dread.

1. **Scene 1: the plan (timed choice).** Every shutter in the mall drops. The only route to the
   service tunnels runs through the Food Court. The four pick a way in.
2. **Food Court.** Fryer smoke, sticky tile, a fountain full of drool. The plan you picked changes
   the crowd: sealed gate, PA lure, full charge, or no plan at all.
3. **Kitchen door.** Past the walk-in freezer, into the back.
4. **Scene 2: Ray vs the locker (timed choice).** Ray the pizza cook is trapped behind the counter.
   Behind him, the Security Locker, the last heavy gear in the mall, is about to fire-seal.
5. **Kitchen boss room.** The Deep-Fry Butcher is working the prep line. He has been for hours.
6. **Scene 3: boss intro.** The Butcher roars. What the survivors say next depends on Ray.
7. **Boss fight.** Dodge the cleaver, dodge the fry-basket swings, and put the Butcher in his own oil.
8. **Cliffhanger.** The fryers die. The Grease Main gauge does not. Back in the Security Room, the
   door log finally prints: FRONT DOORS OPENED 4:59 AM. KEYCARD: MALL COP.

## 4. Wings 2 to 6 (Outline Only)

Each wing is fed by the Grease Main. Each boss was somebody's job title.

- **Wing 2: Arcade, Bowling & Batting Cages.** The machines are still singing their attract loops.
  The lane-oil machine is plugged into the Grease Main. Boss: **Gutterball, the Lane King**.
- **Wing 3: Movie Theater.** Every seat is taken. Nobody is watching the screen. The butter pump
  never stopped. Boss: **The Projectionist**.
- **Wing 4: Fitness Center.** The smoothie bar served a "Fry-Protein" shake. The trainers have
  gone very personal. Boss: **Max Rep**.
- **Wing 5: Toy Store & Holiday Atrium.** The chestnut cart roasted all night. The line to sit on
  Santa's lap never moved. Boss: **Santa Claws**.
- **Wing 6: Hardware & Garden Center, then Rooftop Helipad.** The Grease Main drains into the
  garden center's compost. Something grew. Then a way off the roof. Boss: **Mulchmaw**.

Open thread for later wings, answer reserved: whose hand swiped the Mall Cop's keycard at 4:59?

## 5. Survivors

Voice rule: each one must be identifiable from a single line with the name covered.

### Biker (Tank, chainsaw)

- **Backstory:** Rode his motorcycle through the parking lot to get a family to their car, then
  rode it through the front doors when he ran out of parking lot. Says the helmet is "at home."
- **Voice:** Short. Loud. Engine words. Never uses a name, only nicknames: the Mall Cop is
  "Badge," the Skater is "Kid," the Mall Goth is "Spooky," Ray is "Pizza."
  - "Stairs? I don't do stairs. Stairs do me."
  - "Quiet's for libraries and the dead. I'm neither."
  - "Easy, Badge. I only cut what's already dead. Mostly."
- **Motivation:** One fair fight with something that stays down.

### Mall Cop (Ranged, pump shotgun)

- **Backstory:** Eleven years on mall patrol. Zero real arrests. Knows every shutter code, every
  blind spot and every pretzel lady by name. His keycard opened the doors at 4:59. He doesn't know.
- **Voice:** Formal, procedural, radio-speak. Cites the handbook by section when scared. Says
  "copy," "negative," "for the record."
  - "Per Section Nine, nobody gets eaten in my mall. That includes you."
  - "Copy. Shotgun's loaded, shutters are logged, and I am very calm."
  - "For the record, I objected. Into my radio. Loudly."
- **Motivation:** Finish one shift without losing a single customer. That ship sailed. He's
  bailing it out with a bucket.

### Skater (Speed, spiked bat + dash)

- **Backstory:** Banned from this mall three times for skating the atrium after close. Knows
  every rail, ramp and loading dock. Came back today for the doorbuster. Mostly for the rails.
- **Voice:** Fast, slangy, run-on, half-laughing. "Dude" is punctuation. Narrates like a trick.
  - "Dude. Dude. That escalator is eating a guy. That's so gnarly. I hate it."
  - "Bat, dash, bonk, gone. That's the whole plan, dude."
  - "Ten seconds? Dude, I've landed tricks in less. Mostly."
- **Motivation:** Prove the mall is still a place for good times, even if it kills them.

### Mall Goth (AOE: hairspray torch, spray-paint bombs, boombox)

- **Backstory:** Worked the record store and the perfume counter, both on commission. Has been
  waiting her whole life for the world to end. Is annoyed that it ended at a mall.
- **Voice:** Dry, deadpan, theatrical. Finds the dead aesthetically disappointing. Calls them
  "the customers." Never raises her voice except for Ray.
  - "Hairspray and a lighter. Cheaper than therapy, and it works faster."
  - "The customers smell like a fryer that died. Twice."
  - "If we're going down, we're going down to my music."
- **Motivation:** Be the last cool thing standing in this mall.

## 6. Ray (NPC)

Ray runs the pizza counter. Flour to the elbows, a spatula he will not put down, and the belief
that every problem is solved with more garlic. He knows every survivor's order. He has slipped the
Mall Goth an extra garlic knot every closing shift for a year, and she has never once said thanks.
He is the heart of the Food Court. What happens to him is the first thing the party carries.

## 8. Scene 2: Ray vs the Locker (choice id `rayLocker`)

**Prompt:** "Ray is cornered. The Security Locker is about to seal."

### Setup lines (5)

1. **skater:** "Dude, that's Ray! Pizza Ray! Behind the counter, and he's still got all his parts!"
2. **ray:** "HEY! Over here! I make the pizza! Nobody eats the pizza guy! That's the rule!"
3. **mallCop:** "The Security Locker's behind him. Last heavy gear in the mall. Fire seals it in seconds."
4. **mallGoth:** "He's slipped me extra garlic knots for a year. We are not leaving him."
5. **biker:** "Can't do both, Spooky. Pizza or the guns. Pick before the fire does."

### Option labels (buttons)

| pick | label | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| save | Save Ray! | Ray saved; locker burns. |
| loot | Loot the Security Locker. | Heavy gear; Ray is overrun and dies. |
| hold | Try to hold the shutter! | Ray lives but injured; sprinter ambush. |
| silence | ... | Ray lost; only scrap from the locker. |

### Replies (exactly 3 each)

- **save**
  - **skater:** "Ray! Grab my hand! Do NOT let go, dude!"

  - **mallGoth:** "The locker's ash. Worth it. He still smells like garlic."
- **loot**
  - **mallCop:** "Grab the gear! Move, move! That's an order!"

  - **mallGoth:** "I'm never going to forget that sound. Neither are you."

  - **biker:** "Shoulders in! Get under it, Pizza! I got maybe three seconds!"
  - **ray:** "AGH! My leg! It's caught, it's caught!"

- **silence**
  - **mallCop:** "...We froze. Eleven years on the job, and I froze."

## 9. Scene 3: Boss Intro (The Deep-Fry Butcher)

**Title card:** THE DEEP-FRY BUTCHER / FOOD COURT KITCHEN

**Roar (all variants):** "ORDER UP! FRESH MEAT SPECIAL! FOUR-PIECE, EXTRA CRISPY!"

Delivered like a counter yell over a fryer, cleaver raised like he's ringing a bell.

### hopeful (Ray saved)

- **ray:** "That's Dale from the carvery! I'll sling hot slices from the back. Keep him busy!"
- **mallCop:** "Negative, Ray. Civilians stay behind the sneeze guard."

### guilt (locker looted, Ray overrun)

- **skater:** "Hope those shotgun shells were worth it."
- **mallGoth:** "..."
- **mallCop:** "They'll have to be."

### strained (Ray injured)

- **ray:** "Can't stand on this leg. Just... don't die on me, okay?"

- **mallCop:** "No cook. No backup. Just us and the fryer."

## 13. Recap / Cliffhanger

The Butcher goes into Fryer Eight and the kitchen finally goes quiet. Ray is safe, limping or
gone, and the party carries that into the dark. But the fryers were never the source: down in the
sub-basement, the Grease Main gauge keeps climbing, pushing bad oil toward the arcade, the theater,
the gym, the atrium and the garden center. Back in the Security Room, the jammed door printer
finally spits out its log. FRONT DOORS OPENED 4:59 AM. KEYCARD: MALL COP. He stares at it. "That's
my card. I never..." Upstairs, in the arcade, every machine flips to the same high-score screen.
